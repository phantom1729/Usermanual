1. Initial Setup: Connecting Your Guardian

For Raksha to send background audio, video, and live location during an emergency, it needs to be linked to your guardian's Telegram account.

Follow these exact steps to link the app:

  1. Open Telegram on your Guardian's phone (parents, trusted relatives, or friends).
  2. Find their Chat ID: Search for the bot @userinfobot on Telegram and send the message /start. The bot will reply with an "ID" (a string of numbers). Copy this ID.
  3. Start the Raksha Bot: On your guardian's Telegram, search for our official alert bot: @Raksha_Aryan_Bot and press /start.
  4. Configure the App: Open the Raksha app, tap on System Preferences (the gear icon), and enter the following:
    • Display Name: Your name (so guardians know who is alerting them).
    • Secure Chat ID: Paste the ID you copied in Step 2.
    • Emergency Contact: Enter your guardian's standard phone number.
  5. Save Preferences: Tap the save button. The system will refresh and apply your configuration.
Crucial Step: Step 3 is mandatory! If your guardian does not press start on the @Raksha_Aryan_Bot, Telegram's privacy settings will block the emergency alerts from reaching them.

2. Core Features Explained

SOS Emergency

The primary trigger. Pressing this instantly starts recording audio and video from both cameras, fetches your live GPS coordinates, and continuously broadcasts them to the configured Telegram Chat ID.

Fake Power Off Mode

Tapping this instantly turns your screen pitch-black, mimicking a dead battery or powered-off phone. Your screen will become unresponsive to normal touches, but background SOS tracking and recording will continue seamlessly.

How to Exit: Rapidly tap the black screen 6 times to unlock it.

Guard Mode

When activated, this arms the device's accelerometer. If the phone detects a violent shake or drop, it will automatically trigger the SOS Emergency protocol without requiring a screen tap.

Simulate Call

Triggers a highly realistic, interactive fake phone call from the "Police Department." It features a ringing screen, an active call timer, and a scripted voice dialogue from a dispatcher to deter threats.

Guardian Angel Network

A community safety feature. If another Raksha user triggers an SOS within a nearby radius, you will receive a silent alert allowing you to navigate to them and help.

3. How to Test Your Setup (Dry Run)

We highly recommend testing the app to ensure your guardian receives the data correctly.

  1. Inform your guardian that you are conducting a test.
  2. Open the app and tap SOS Emergency.
  3. Let it run for 15-20 seconds to allow the camera and GPS to capture data.
  4. Tap the screen 6 times to disarm (if in stealth mode) or close the app.
  5. Check your guardian's Telegram (@Raksha_Aryan_Bot) to verify that the location link, battery status, and media files were successfully delivered.
